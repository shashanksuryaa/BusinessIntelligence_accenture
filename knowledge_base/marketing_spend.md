# Marketing Spend KPI

## Definition
Marketing Spend represents expenditure on marketing and advertising
activities.

## Business Role
Marketing Spend is a controllable demand-generation lever.

## Related Drivers
- Orders
- Conversion Rate
- Revenue

## Business Levers
- Campaign allocation
- Channel budget
- Audience targeting
- Campaign optimization

## Investigation Guidance
When marketing spend changes materially, investigate:
- Campaign launches
- Budget changes
- Channel allocation
- Audience targeting
- Conversion performance

## Interpretation
Marketing spend may be associated with demand and revenue changes,
but increased spend does not automatically imply increased revenue.

## Recommended Actions
Strong evidence:
- Review campaign performance.
- Compare spend changes with conversion and order movements.

Weak evidence:
- Monitor campaign performance.
- Avoid increasing spend solely based on correlation.

## Caution
Marketing spend and revenue can move together without establishing
a causal relationship.
