# Marketing Persona

## Objective
Marketing teams need insight into demand generation, conversion
and campaign-related signals.

## Preferred Drivers
- Marketing Spend
- Conversion Rate
- Orders
- Discount Rate
- AOV

## Preferred Actions
- Review campaign performance
- Investigate audience targeting
- Review conversion funnel
- Compare campaign timing with KPI movement

## Low Confidence
Recommend campaign investigation or monitoring rather than changing
budget based on weak evidence.

## Causality
Do not treat correlation between marketing activity and revenue as
proof that marketing caused the movement.
