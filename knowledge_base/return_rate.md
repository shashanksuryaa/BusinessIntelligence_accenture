# Return Rate KPI

## Definition
Return Rate represents the proportion of completed orders that are
subsequently returned.

## Business Role
Return Rate is a customer and product-quality indicator.

## Related Drivers
- Product quality
- Product mix
- Discount Rate
- Customer experience

## Business Levers
- Product quality
- Product descriptions
- Customer support
- Return policy
- Product selection

## Investigation Guidance
When return rate changes materially, investigate:
- Product categories
- Product quality
- Customer complaints
- Regional patterns
- Promotion-related purchases

## Recommended Actions
Strong evidence:
- Investigate affected products and categories.
- Review customer feedback and return reasons.

Weak evidence:
- Monitor return trends.
- Investigate before changing policy.

## Caution
A relationship between returns and revenue does not establish causality.
