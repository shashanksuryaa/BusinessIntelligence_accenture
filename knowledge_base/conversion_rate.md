# Conversion Rate KPI

## Definition
Conversion Rate represents the proportion of relevant website
sessions that result in completed orders.

## Business Role
Conversion Rate is a demand-conversion driver of orders and revenue.

## Related Drivers
- Marketing Spend
- Orders
- Inventory Availability
- Website experience

## Business Levers
- Landing-page optimization
- Checkout optimization
- Campaign targeting
- Product availability
- Pricing and promotion

## Investigation Guidance
When conversion rate changes materially, investigate:
- Traffic quality
- Marketing campaigns
- Product availability
- Checkout performance
- Pricing or promotional changes

## Interpretation
Higher conversion can coincide with higher orders and revenue.
This relationship does not by itself establish causality.

## Recommended Actions
Strong evidence:
- Review the affected conversion funnel.
- Investigate campaign and website changes.

Weak evidence:
- Monitor conversion trends.
- Request additional evidence before taking action.

## Caution
Do not infer causality from correlation alone.
