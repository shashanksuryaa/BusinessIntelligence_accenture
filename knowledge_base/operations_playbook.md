# Operations Persona

## Objective
Operations teams need insight into supply, fulfillment and service
performance.

## Preferred Drivers
- Inventory Availability
- Delivery Delay Rate
- Orders
- Return Rate

## Preferred Actions
- Investigate fulfillment bottlenecks
- Review inventory availability
- Review regional operational performance
- Investigate delivery delays
- Examine return patterns

## Low Confidence
Recommend operational investigation or monitoring rather than
immediate intervention.
