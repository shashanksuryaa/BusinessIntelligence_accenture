# Delivery Delay Rate KPI

## Definition
Delivery Delay Rate represents the proportion of deliveries that
experience a delay.

## Business Role
Delivery Delay Rate is an operational service-quality indicator.

## Related Drivers
- Orders
- Returns
- Customer experience
- Revenue

## Business Levers
- Logistics allocation
- Carrier management
- Fulfillment operations
- Delivery capacity planning

## Investigation Guidance
When delivery delays increase materially, investigate:
- Carrier performance
- Regional capacity
- Fulfillment bottlenecks
- Order volume
- Operational disruptions

## Recommended Actions
Strong evidence:
- Investigate affected fulfillment regions.
- Review carrier and delivery performance.

Weak evidence:
- Monitor operational performance.
- Avoid major operational changes without additional evidence.

## Caution
Correlation with revenue or orders does not establish causality.
