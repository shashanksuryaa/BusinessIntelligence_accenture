# Orders KPI

## Definition
Orders represents the number of completed customer orders.

## Business Role
Orders is a primary volume driver of revenue.

## Related Drivers
- Conversion Rate
- Inventory Availability
- Marketing Spend
- Delivery Delay Rate

## Business Levers
- Campaign targeting
- Conversion optimization
- Product availability
- Checkout optimization
- Demand generation

## Investigation Guidance
When orders change materially, investigate:
- Conversion rate
- Marketing activity
- Inventory availability
- Website or checkout issues
- Delivery constraints

## Interpretation
A change in orders can be associated with a change in revenue, but
association does not establish causality.

## Recommended Actions
Strong evidence:
- Investigate the operational or commercial factors associated with
  the order movement.
- Consider targeted demand-generation actions when supported by
  additional evidence.

Weak evidence:
- Monitor the metric.
- Investigate before taking significant action.

## Caution
Do not claim that orders caused a revenue movement solely from
correlation or historical association.
