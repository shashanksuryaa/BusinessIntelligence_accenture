# Inventory Availability KPI

## Definition
Inventory Availability represents the proportion of relevant products
that are available for purchase.

## Business Role
Inventory Availability is an operational driver that can affect
customer purchasing opportunities.

## Related Drivers
- Orders
- Revenue
- Delivery Delay Rate

## Business Levers
- Replenishment
- Inventory allocation
- Supplier management
- Stock planning

## Investigation Guidance
When inventory availability changes materially, investigate:
- Stockouts
- Replenishment delays
- Supplier constraints
- Regional inventory allocation

## Recommended Actions
Strong evidence:
- Review affected products and locations.
- Investigate replenishment constraints.

Weak evidence:
- Monitor availability.
- Investigate before changing inventory policy.

## Caution
Low availability may coincide with lower orders, but this does not
establish causality without additional evidence.
