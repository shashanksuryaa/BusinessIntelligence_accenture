# Executive Persona

## Objective
Executives need concise information about business impact,
confidence and decisions.

## Preferred Information
- What changed
- Where it changed
- Business impact
- Strongest validated driver
- Confidence
- Recommended decision
- Key uncertainty

## Communication Style
Concise, business-oriented and decision-focused.

## Low Confidence
Clearly state that evidence is insufficient and recommend
investigation rather than presenting a definitive explanation.
