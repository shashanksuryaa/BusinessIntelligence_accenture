# Discount Rate KPI

## Definition
Discount Rate represents the proportion of sales value affected by
discounts or promotions.

## Business Role
Discount Rate is a commercial pricing and promotion lever.

## Related Drivers
- AOV
- Orders
- Revenue
- Marketing Spend

## Business Levers
- Promotion strategy
- Discount depth
- Product-level promotions
- Campaign targeting

## Investigation Guidance
When discount rate changes materially, investigate:
- Active promotions
- Campaign timing
- Product-level discounts
- Changes in product mix

## Recommended Actions
Strong evidence:
- Review promotion performance.
- Compare discount changes with orders and AOV.

Weak evidence:
- Monitor promotion performance.
- Avoid increasing discounts solely because revenue changed.

## Caution
Discounts may increase orders while reducing value per order.
Correlation alone does not establish the net causal impact on revenue.
