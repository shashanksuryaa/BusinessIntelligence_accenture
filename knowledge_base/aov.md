# Average Order Value KPI

## Definition
Average Order Value represents average revenue generated per completed
order.

## Business Role
AOV is a revenue value driver.

## Related Drivers
- Pricing
- Discount Rate
- Product Mix
- Orders

## Business Levers
- Cross-selling
- Upselling
- Product bundling
- Pricing
- Promotion design

## Investigation Guidance
When AOV changes materially, investigate:
- Product mix
- Discounting
- Pricing changes
- Premium-product sales
- Basket composition

## Interpretation
AOV changes can influence revenue independently of order volume.

## Recommended Actions
Strong evidence:
- Investigate product and basket mix.
- Review pricing and promotion changes.

Weak evidence:
- Monitor AOV and product mix before changing pricing strategy.

## Caution
A correlation between AOV and revenue does not establish causality.
